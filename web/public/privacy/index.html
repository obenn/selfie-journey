YOUR STORY DESERVES CARE · SEPTEMBER 2026

A little clarity
about privacy.

Your portraits are personal. Here’s what stays with you, what you can choose to share, and how to change your mind.

Your photos, journal notes, face landmarks, and camera frames are never sent to our feedback or telemetry service. Face guidance and lookback films are processed on your device.

Your journal and camera

Your active journal lives in the app’s local storage on your iPhone or iPad. Camera access is requested when you take a portrait. Importing with Apple’s photo picker gives the app the image you choose, rather than access to your whole library.

The camera uses Apple’s Vision framework and image brightness samples for framing and lighting advice. This is face detection, not identity recognition. We don’t store face templates or a history of your facial landmarks.

iCloud backups

When enabled, dated snapshots copy your portraits, notes, dates, and chosen frames to the app’s container in your own iCloud Drive. This uses your Apple Account and iCloud storage. Backups can be turned off in settings.

Deleting a portrait from the local journal does not delete older iCloud snapshots. That history makes missing-day recovery possible. Turning backups off stops new backups; it does not erase copies already in iCloud. Apple manages iCloud storage and transfer. Uploads require an available account, connection, and storage.

Telemetry: your choice

Selfie Journey uses basic product telemetry to understand which features are useful and where technical issues occur. Full telemetry is enabled by default. You can select Off, Limited, or Full in the app’s settings at any time.

Your settingWhat is sent
OffNo product telemetry is sent.
LimitedAggregate counts of the same basic app events, without an installation identifier, app version, operating system version, or device class. These help us understand overall feature use.
FullBasic app events, app version, operating system version, device class, and a random installation identifier. The identifier helps estimate active installations; it is not your name or Apple Account.

Telemetry does not include portraits, videos, journal notes, face data, precise location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or your Apple Account. We do not use an advertising analytics SDK or sell this information. Turning telemetry off stops future telemetry; it does not automatically remove information already received.

Feedback and optional diagnostics

When you send feedback, we receive the category, message, submission time, and an email address if you choose to include one. An email is used only to respond to your message, not to add you to a mailing list.

Feedback sent from the app can include a diagnostic attachment if you choose. You can review it before sending. It contains a small set of predefined technical event codes and their approximate ages, along with app version, operating system version, and device class. It does not contain your portraits, journal notes, camera frames, face landmarks, or unrestricted system logs.

Your telemetry setting and diagnostic attachment are separate choices. You may send feedback with telemetry off. The website’s feedback form sends no diagnostic attachment. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in the message itself.

Storage and retention

Our feedback and telemetry service runs on Cloudflare. Administrative access is protected with Cloudflare Access. Cloudflare processes network information needed to deliver and secure requests.

  • Feedback messages and optional contact emails are retained for up to 180 days.
  • Optional diagnostic attachments and pseudonymous Full telemetry events are retained for up to 30 days.
  • Anonymous aggregate counts are retained for up to 365 days.
  • Local portraits and your iCloud backups follow the storage behavior described above, separately from these service retention periods.

Retention limits are enforced by scheduled cleanup. Data may remain until the next scheduled cleanup runs.

This website

The public website does not run marketing trackers, advertising scripts, or product analytics. Its feedback form sends only the information you submit. The private admin dashboard uses Cloudflare Access authentication, which may use cookies for its signed-in session.

Permissions and control

Daily reminders are local notifications that you can enable, change, or disable. Camera and notification permissions can also be managed in iOS Settings. You can change telemetry, backups, portrait framing, and your reminder time in the app.

If you would like to ask about information you submitted or request deletion of feedback, send us a note and include the feedback reference if you have it. Avoid sending additional personal details unless needed to identify the submission.

Changes and questions

We’ll update these notes when the app’s data practices change. Questions are welcome through Support & feedback.
