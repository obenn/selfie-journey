Is Selfie Journey really free?

Yes. Selfie Journey is completely free, with no subscriptions. Taking daily portraits, building your streak, and creating lookback films are all included.

Where are my portraits stored?

Your active journal is stored on your device. With iCloud backup enabled, dated copies also live in your own iCloud Drive. Your portraits are not sent to our feedback or telemetry service.

How do I change reminders or my portrait frame?

Tap the settings button on the Today screen. You can choose a different reminder time, turn reminders off, or select any of the four portrait frames.

Can I turn off telemetry?

Yes. In the app’s settings, choose Off, Limited, or Full. Full is the default. Feedback and its optional diagnostic attachment are separate from this preference.
