A little context helps.

For a technical issue, describe what happened and what you expected. You can choose to mention your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.

The app sends no logs or device information. GitHub issues may be public, so please leave out personal photos, journal notes, passwords, and other private details.

Is Selfie Journey really free?

Yes. Selfie Journey is completely free, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases. Daily portraits, streaks, and lookback films are all included.

Where are my portraits stored?

Your journal lives on your device. With iCloud backup enabled, dated copies also live in your own private iCloud Drive. We cannot access those backups.

Does the app collect analytics?

No. The current app has no analytics, installation identifiers, or diagnostic uploads. Face guidance and film creation happen on your device.

How do I change reminders or my portrait frame?

Tap the settings button on Today. Choose a different reminder time, turn reminders off, or select any of the four portrait frames.
